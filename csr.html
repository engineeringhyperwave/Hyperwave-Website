Corporate Social Responsibility

At HYPERWAVE, we believe in giving back to the communities where we do business. With the goal of making a positive impact in the lives of the communities in which we operate, we actively engage in activities that appropriately address the needs of our local communities, as well as the needs of our future generations.

We also believe that corporate responsibility is a virtuous cycle where our support helps to build and grow sustainable environments in which everyone prospers. We will continuously support meaningful programs that help give back to the communities and build a healthy society.

Rumah Anak Yatim dan Miskin Islam Paya Rumput Jawa (PAYASUM), Sg. Udang, Melaka ▼

In February 2019 HYPERWAVE organized a CSR activity at our Melaka Branch office with PAYASUM. PAYASUM which manages a total of 66 under privileged children is the oldest orphanage operating in the state of Melaka. HYPERWAVE donated basic necessity needs such as tables, items for laundry use (detergents, ironing boards and irons), kitchen cleaning supplies, household cleaning supplies and first aid medical supplies to support the continual operations of PAYASUM. A dinner get together was also organized at the Melaka Office and each child was given books and stationeries to encourage better learning at school.

Rumah Anak Yatim PERKIM, Merchang, Terengganu ▼

In February 2019 HYPERWAVE organized a CSR activity to renovate and repair the facility at Rumah Anak Yatim PERKIM at Merchang. Amongst the renovation works that were carried out were to renovate and repair the Surau’s floor, painting of the warden’s quarters, electrical repair works with new lighting and fans and new mosquito on the children’s hostels and plumbing repair works on the children’s shower area.

Caring For the Future Malaysia, Papar, Sabah ▼

In October – December 2018, HYPERWAVE organized several CSR activities with Caring for the Future Malaysia (CFFM). CFFM is an authorized non-profit organization operating a children’s home for the orphaned, abused and neglected children in Sabah. The CSR activities with CFFM are Electrical upgrading works for the facility, day trip outing to Kota Kinabalu city for the children and provided Christmas presents to the children of the foundation.

In October 18 HYPERWAVE organized a CSR activity with Thafiz children of Raudah Ribat As-Syafie, Dungun. Masjid Ali Al-Hasan, Tulungagung, East Java, Indonesia ▼

In September 2018 HYPERWAVE organized a CSR activity and reached out to the rural area of Tulungagung, East Java. Hyperwave sponsored construction materials such as re-bars, sand, granite, wood, cement etc. to renovate and extend the floor area of the mosque. The new area will provide a more comfortable environment for the students to perform religious studies. The CSR brought closeness to the community as it was a gotong-royong activity by the local community. New carpets, books and other stationeries were also donated to the mosque.

Sekolah Kebangsaan Ulu Pelagus, Kapit District ▼

In November 2017 Hyperwave reached out to support Sekolah Kebangsaan Ulu Pelagus, a rural school that serves the community of Kapit District. The surrounding community and the school itself do not have electrical power supply, running water and landline phone communication. It solely depends on a diesel powered generator and river as its main resources of energy and water. Its ultra-remote location does not even receive mobile phone signal. Access to the school and its community is either via off road trail or boat ride up Kapit River. Hyperwave performed facility upgrading such as repair works on the elevated platform and existing structure, provided new electrical appliances such as fans, LED lights, air-conditioning units and kitchen appliances, new pillows and mattresses for the student’s hostel, pre-school student’s tables and chairs, books, stationeries, dry-fit T-shirts, personal hygiene cleaning supplies, and constructed a new badminton court for the school.

Majilis Berbuka Puasa with Kebajikan Anak-Anak Yatim Kampung Jepak, Bintulu ▼

The event started off with the breaking fast prayer followed by the get together dinner with the children. After dinner, the children were treated with a fireworks show and they were given sparklers to play with. At the end of the event, each child received school and sport clothing, a pair each of school and sport shoes and some duit raya.
